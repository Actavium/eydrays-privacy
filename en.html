EYDRAYS — Privacy notice

9 November 2026 · EYDRAYS 0.10

Provider: a private individual (independent developer of EYDRAYS). Contact for support and privacy questions: watchthelimit@outlook.com

An imagined future

EYDRAYS is a visualization tool. Messages, balances, transfers, bookings and invitations are fictional. It does not connect to bank accounts, process money, make bookings, provide investment advice or guarantee outcomes. Real places may be mentioned as context; fictional correspondents do not represent those businesses.

What stays on your device

The app stores your selected language, currency, interests, design, notification preferences, onboarding state and a random local planning seed. Local fictional story state records recurring characters, simulated events and their relationships. It also stores simulated account transactions and balances, planned notification content and times, received messages, reading status and references to bundled photographs. This information is used to run and personalize your local experience. No registration, real financial credentials, contacts, precise location, camera or microphone access is requested.

Local storage and retention

Preferences, plans and simulated accounting are stored in app-private SQLite storage. Received messages are stored in a separate app-private SQLite mailbox; Android and Notifee also hold scheduled and displayed notification content. Received mailbox records and past planning records are retained for up to 365 days during normal app housekeeping. Simulated ledger history remains until you delete the app data. Storage is protected by Android app isolation; this is not a claim of separately encrypted database contents. Android device backup is disabled in this release.

Notifications and visibility

You choose whether to permit Android notifications and activate daily delivery. Native approximate alarms work without an exact-alarm special permission. Android may delay or suppress delivery and controls lock-screen previews and pop-ups. People who can see your screen or notification history may see message text. Pause cancels future notifications; it does not delete received history. Displayed notifications can be dismissed in Android.

Network and external services

This release bundles its messages and photos and does not use an account server, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, cloud AI, remote push service or live exchange-rate service. Its Android release manifest does not grant Internet permission, so app processes cannot send your locally stored information over the network. If you open a photo source or licence link, Android opens an external browser or app. That provider receives the normal request information under its own privacy terms; EYDRAYS does not attach your mailbox or simulated ledger. Google Play, Android and your device manufacturer may independently process installation, security or diagnostic information under their own settings and policies.

Deleting your information

To remove all locally stored EYDRAYS information, use Android Settings → Apps → EYDRAYS → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. This also removes your local progress and is irreversible unless you have independently retained a copy. No remote EYDRAYS account or server copy exists to delete. Clearing local data does not erase screenshots or other copies you made outside the app.

Choices, contact and changes

You can change interests, design, language, currency and notification choices in Settings without creating an account. There is no advertising, payment or subscription. If you later contact support, only send information you choose to share. Future versions that change data handling will need an updated notice.
