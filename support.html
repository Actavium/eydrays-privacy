EYDRAYS — Support

Questions, problems or feedback: watchthelimit@outlook.com. EYDRAYS is an offline visualization app by an independent developer; letters, bookings, purchases and balances are fictional.

Letters do not arrive

Open Settings → Notifications in EYDRAYS, allow notifications and resume daily delivery. Two to four letters arrive per day within your active hours. On iPhone, also check Settings → Notifications → EYDRAYS and any Focus mode. Open the app at least once a week so it can plan the following days.

Deleting your data

All data stays on your device. On iPhone, delete the app (offloading keeps its data). On Android, use Settings → Apps → EYDRAYS → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall.
